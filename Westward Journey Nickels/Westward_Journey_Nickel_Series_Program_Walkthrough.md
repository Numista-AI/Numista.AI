# Walkthrough — Westward Journey Nickel Series™ (2004–2005) Research & Program Analysis

## Summary of Accomplishments

### 1. Researched the 2004–2005 Nickel Redesigns
Conducted an authoritative investigation using live U.S. Mint registries (`usmint.gov/learn/coins-and-medals/circulating-coins/nickel/westward-journey-nickels`) and Congressional statutes:
- **Statutory Authority:** Authorized by Congress under the *American 5-Cent Coin Design Continuity Act of 2003* (Public Law 108-15; 31 U.S.C. § 5112(p)).
- **Program Scope:** 2-year circulating multi-coin commemorative program commemorating the bicentennials of the **Louisiana Purchase (1803)** and the **Lewis & Clark Expedition (1804–1806)**.
- **4 Distinct Reverse Designs & 2 Obverse Designs:**
  1. **2004 Peace Medal** (Louisiana Purchase — clasped hands and crossed pipe/tomahawk).
  2. **2004 Keelboat** (55-foot expedition vessel under full sail on the Missouri River).
  3. **2005 American Bison / Buffalo** (Majestic western bison on grassy rise; features one-year-only close-up Jefferson portrait with handwritten "Liberty" script).
  4. **2005 Ocean in View!** (Pacific Ocean coastline with Clark's famous quote: *"Ocean in view! O! The joy!"*).
  5. **2006 Permanent Redesign:** Return to Monticello on reverse, paired with a brand-new forward-facing Jefferson obverse.
