✦ Import Guide

Your personal AI guide for loading coins, invoices, & spreadsheets

👋 Hi! I'm Morgan. Follow these 2 easy steps to upload your coin records or invoices:

Step 1 — What to Click:

Click the blue 📁 Browse Files or Folder button below (or drag and drop your files into the box).

Step 2 — How to Find & Search for Your Invoice File:
  • Where files live: Open your computer's Downloads or Documents folder in the file selection popup window.
  • Search by Store/Dealer Name: In the search box at the top-right of your file manager window, type your seller's name (e.g. US Mint, Littleton, APMEX, eBay, GreatCollections, Heritage, or Receipt).
  • File Types Accepted: PDF Invoices (.pdf), Excel/CSV spreadsheets (.xlsx, .csv), or coin photos/scans (.jpg, .png).

🔍 Morgan's Quick Tips for Dealer Invoices:

🏛️ US Mint Orders:

Search USM or US Mint in Downloads. Or log into usmint.gov → Order History → Save Order Details as PDF.

📫 Littleton / Dealer Scans:

Search Scan_ or Receipt_ in your Downloads directory.

🛍️ APMEX / eBay / Heritage:

Save purchase confirmation emails as PDF (Ctrl+P → Save as PDF), then select that PDF here.
