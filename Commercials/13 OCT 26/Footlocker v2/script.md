# Numista.Ai — Cartoon Commercial #1 v2: "The Footlocker & The Video Game Bucks"
Thanksgiving Facebook ad · 9:16 master · ~30 s · 7 shots + end card · 2D Owl Family style
Changes from v1: game-currency line replaced with "video game bucks" (no game or publisher names) · 2D Owl Family style instead of Pixar 3D · brand "Numista.Ai" · new tagline · added Morgan-does-the-work shots (5–6) in Gram Pa's footlocker scene, before the wink and logo. No values, no readable coin names/dates, no PII, no brand logos on devices.

## Style line (append to every still and motion prompt)
2D hand-drawn cartoon, Owl Family style: warm gold and bronze owls with soft painted shading, clean ink outlines, big round circuit-pattern teal eyes, navy-blue shadows, cozy storybook look, flat 2D animation, not 3D, not photoreal, no text, no logos, 9:16 vertical, full-bleed, no borders.

## Continuity rules (append to every still and motion prompt)
- DEVICES: a phone or tablet's screen faces its user, and its back faces everyone else. The camera sees a screen only from over the user's shoulder. No character ever looks at the back of their own device.
- DEVICE BACKS: whenever a device back is visible, it shows only a small, simple Cyber Owl coin logo (ref: Cyber Owl coin PNG), sized like a device-maker logo, with rim text too small to read. Never Apple or any other brand.
- WARDROBE: Nicky always wears his teal hoodie and his orange-rimmed headphones around his neck. Momma always wears her cream cardigan. Gram Pa always wears his brown cardigan sweater, clearly visible.

## Cast
- Gram Pa: older grey-silver owl, round thin gold wire glasses, brown knit cardigan.
- Momma Owl: golden-tan owl, round gold glasses, cream knit cardigan.
- Nicky (15): teen OWL (beak, feathers, not human), orange-rimmed headphones around his neck, teal hoodie.
- Morgan: exactly morgan_avatar.png (golden AI owl in a gold coin rim on navy, teal circuit eyes). Appears only on the phone screen.
