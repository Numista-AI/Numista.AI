## Policy Reminders

- **AI Generation:** HOLD. Do not create or accept AI renders of coins or notes for the reference library.
- **Allowed sources only:** Official U.S. Mint, NNC/Smithsonian, BEP, LOC, NARA, Newman Numismatic Portal (public-domain items), clearly licensed Wikimedia.
- **Prohibited:** Heritage, Stack’s, PMG/PCGS scrapes, auction photography, Kaggle training sets, AI.
