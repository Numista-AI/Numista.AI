⚠️ The Crisis

The "Shoebox Dilemma" & The Silver Tsunami

Over the next 20 years, the greatest generational wealth transfer in human history will take place. Millions of aging collectors hold lifelong treasures that are completely undocumented.

📦
Undocumented Safe Boxes
Over 70% of senior collectors store their collections in safety deposit boxes, binder albums, and cigar boxes with handwritten notes or unrecorded mental knowledge.
📉
The 95% Liquidation Loss
When collectors pass, overwhelmed heirs often take rare key-date coins ($5,000–$50,000 values) to local pawn shops, getting paid only melt silver/gold spot price ($20–$30).
⚔️
Estate Attorney Gridlock
Physical collectibles cannot be split like a bank account. Appraisals cost $250/hr, take months, and subjective division leads to bitter family probate disputes.
💡 Why Numismatics is the Hardest AI Problem:
A microscopic die gouge or a difference between an 1881-S Morgan Dollar in MS63 vs MS65 is the difference between $65 and $12,500. Generic vision models fail. It requires specialized multimodal intelligence grounded in certified auction comps.
