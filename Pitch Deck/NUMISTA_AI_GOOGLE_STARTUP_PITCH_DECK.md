## Executive Summary

**Numista.AI** is the world's first AI-native platform designed to solve the **$38 Billion "Shoebox Dilemma"** — the massive, uncatalogued physical coin and currency collections owned by the aging Baby Boomer generation ("The Silver Tsunami"). 

As **$84 Trillion** in generational wealth transfers over the next two decades, senior collectors risk having their lifelong treasures liquidated by inexperienced heirs at 5% of their true value (e.g. rare key-date coins sold for scrap silver melt value at pawn shops). 

By uniting **Google Vertex AI (Gemini 3.5 Multimodal)**, **Google Document AI**, **Cloud Run**, **Cloud Firestore**, and **Google BigQuery**, Numista.AI provides an accessible, high-empathy ecosystem that:
1. Gives seniors zero-friction cataloging tools (USB microscope auto-capture, invoice/checklist OCR, voice and chat).
2. Introduces **Morgan**, a collection-aware, senior-empathetic AI numismatist powered by Google Vertex AI.
3. Delivers a court-defensible **Estate Planning Engine** with an algorithmic fair-division solver and token-gated **Attorney & Trust Officer Portal**.

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```
- **The Core Problem:**
  1. **Undocumented Safe Boxes:** 70% of senior collectors hold lifelong collections with mental-only knowledge or faded handwritten cards.
  2. **The 95% Liquidation Tragedy:** When collectors pass away, overwhelmed heirs sell $10,000 rare keys for $25 scrap metal value at local pawn shops.
  3. **Estate Attorney Gridlock:** Physical heirlooms cannot be split like bank accounts. Professional appraisals cost $250/hr, take months, and subjective division triggers bitter probate feuds.
- **Why Numismatics is Hard:** A microscopic die gouge or the difference between MS63 and MS65 on an 1881-S Morgan Dollar is the difference between **$65** and **$12,500**. Generic AI fails without specialized multimodal precision.
