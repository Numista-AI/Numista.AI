Executive Summary

The evaluation of the "Sign in with Google" integration and core feature set indicates a strong technical foundation, though key usability frictions exist in the onboarding flow. While core AI identification is performant, environmental factors significantly impact accuracy. Recommendations focus on balancing security, data usage, and UX fluidity to ensure optimal adoption.

UI/UX Design

  • CTA Placement: Needs mobile-focal emphasis on Google sign-in.
  • Feature Weight: "Scan Coin" requires higher visual priority to establish primary value.
  • Onboarding: High friction found. Suggesting "Guest Mode" for demo preview.

Feature Functionality

  • Google Auth: Account merging logic needed for existing email users.
  • AI Accuracy: Sensitive to light reflections in "Proof" coins.
  • Receipt OCR: High risk of inaccurate extraction with handwritten notes.
