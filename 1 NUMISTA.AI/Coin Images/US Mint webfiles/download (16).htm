Shop Official U.S. Mint Products

Browse a variety of official U.S. Mint products that honor American history through the artistry of hand-crafted coins. Discover the perfect pieces for your collection or find a gift for a fellow numismatist in our coins, medals, and coin sets. If you’re looking for the latest releases, check out the 2026 product release schedule and sign up for product subscriptions.

Coin Programs

Authorized by Congress, our coins celebrate and honor American people, places, events, and institutions. These artistic circulating and uncirculated coins, proof sets, and silver, gold, platinum, palladium, and bullion coins are produced by the U.S. Mint and are only available for a limited time.

View All Coin Programs

  • Medals

    The U.S. Mint produces national medals to commemorate significant events and heroes of American history. When legislation permits, bronze duplicates of congressional Gold Medals are struck for sale to the public.
    View All Medals

  • Coin Sets

    Excellent gifts for beginner or experienced collectors, coin sets are produced annually and include the current year’s issues of our most popular coin programs, as well as releases from newer series.
    View All Coin Sets

    Ensure you'll automatically receive all future releases of your favorite U.S. Mint products with the option to cancel any time.
