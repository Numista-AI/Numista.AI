1. Search Heritage Auctions for the specific type/series (e.g., "1918 $1 Federal Reserve Bank Note", "1963A $1 Federal Reserve Star Note", "1934A $1 Hawaii").
2. Choose lots with **professional studio photography** (graded notes in holders usually have the best images).
3. Download the highest-resolution **obverse** and **reverse** images available.
4. Send the files back to me.

I will then:
