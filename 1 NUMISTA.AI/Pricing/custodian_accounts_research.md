## 💡 The Full Feature Concept (As Understood)

Based on everything found, the feature works like this:

| Role | Description |
|---|---|
| **Account Owner** | Your aunt, your father — they have their own Numista.AI login, their own collection, their own PIN |
| **Custodian / Delegate** | You — you have your **own** separate Numista.AI account |
| **The Ask** | The owner grants you "custodian" access to their account. You can then log into your account and **switch into / manage their collection** — uploading coins, managing their inventory — **without ever needing to know their PIN** |

### Why it matters:
- Older collectors may not be tech-savvy but have massive collections
- They shouldn't have to share their PIN or password
- A trusted family member / friend can be designated as a "helper"
- This is different from a "Family Plan" subscription — it's more of a **permission delegation** system
