Coin Production Figures
The Coin Production figures represent all of the "circulating" coins produced by the
	United States Mint, including the coins sold through the numismatic bags & rolls program.
	These numbers do not include the "proof" or "uncirculated" quality
	(i.e. UNC sets) coins produced by the Mint.
