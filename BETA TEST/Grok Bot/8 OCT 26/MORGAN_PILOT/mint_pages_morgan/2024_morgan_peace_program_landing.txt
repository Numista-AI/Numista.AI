The Morgan Silver Dollar
Originally minted from 1878 to 1904, the Morgan Silver Dollar represents our Nation’s westward expansion and industrial development in the 19th century.
Subscribe
The Peace Silver Dollar
The Peace Silver Dollar was originally released in 1921 and symbolizes our Nation making peace with Germany and becoming a world power following World War I.
Subscribe

The Peace Silver Dollar
Own Symbols of Perseverance
The 2025 Morgan and Peace Dollar Two-Coin Reverse Proof Set contains a Morgan Dollar coin and a Peace Dollar coin in stunning reverse proof finishes. Both coins were minted at the San Francisco Mint. The Morgan and Peace Dollar Two-Coin Reverse Proof Set is sure to be a collector favorite for years to come.
