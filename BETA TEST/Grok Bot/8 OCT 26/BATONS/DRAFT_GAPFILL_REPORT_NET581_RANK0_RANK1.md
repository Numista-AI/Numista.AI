## 2. Rank 1: P0-C1 1787 Fugio Cent Specification Draft
**Series:** Confederation / Pre-Federal Coinage  
**Denomination:** One Cent (Copper)  
**Authorized By:** Resolution of the Congress of the Confederation (April 21 & July 6, 1787)  
**Contractor:** James Jarvis (Company for Coining Coppers, New Haven, CT)  
**Status:** First official coin authorized by the United States government under the Articles of Confederation.

### Design & Devices (Morgan Fact-Check Focus)
- **Obverse (Primary Image):**
  - **Device:** Central sundial receiving rays from an overhead sun.
  - **Legends:** FUGIO ("I fly", referencing time) to left of sundial; 1787 at right; exergue inscription MIND YOUR BUSINESS.
  - **Attribution:** Design inspired by Benjamin Franklin's 1776 Continental Currency designs (Franklin inspired, did not personally engrave).
- **Reverse:**
  - **Device:** 13 interlocking circular chain links forming an unbroken endless chain, symbolizing the perpetual union of the 13 American states.
  - **Central Legends:** Central circular boss inscribed WE ARE ONE, surrounded by inner legend ring inscribed AMERICAN CONGRESS (or variant UNITED STATES).

### Primary Major Varieties (Type-Level Distinction)
1. **Regular Circulation Issues (1787):**
   - **Pointed Rays vs. Club Rays:** Pointed rays are dominant; club rays represent distinct obverse hubs.
   - **Reverse Legend Variations:**
     - UNITED STATES (above/below boss) with 4-cinquefoil or cross separators.
     - STATES UNITED (distinct legend arrangement).
2. **Special Varieties & Patterns:**
   - **Fugio with Raised Rims / Concave devices** (Patterns).
   - **New Haven Restrike (c. 1860):** Struck from copy dies by C. Wyllys Betts; narrower rings, distinct high-relief lettering. (Must be classified strictly as restrike/pattern, not 1787 circulation).

### Mintage & Specifications
- **Mintage Figure:** **Disputed / Estimated ~398,000–400,000 pieces** (Jarvis failed to fulfill full contract for 300 tons of copper; Bank of New York hoard of ~1788 discovered in 1856 provided uncirculated specimens).
  - *Rule Compliance:* Mintage marked as **Estimated/Disputed** — no single invented integer.
- **Composition:** 100% Copper (varied purity from Connecticut copper stock).
- **Weight:** ~157.5 grains (~10.2 grams nominal; wide variance).
- **Diameter:** ~28–29 mm.
- **Edge:** Plain.
- **Public Domain Attributable Reference Sources:**
  - National Museum of American History (Smithsonian Institution, National Numismatic Collection).
  - American Numismatic Society (ANS Digital Collection).
  - Library of Congress / Treasury Archives.
