## 1. Rank 6: Thin Evidence Packs Audit (A1–A5, A7)

### A1: Mercury Dimes (Winged Liberty Head, 1916–1945)
- **Designer:** Adolph A. Weinman.
- **Obverse:** Young Liberty in winged Phrygian cap ("Mercury" misnomer; wings symbolize liberty of thought).
- **Reverse:** Roman fasces entwined with olive branch (symbolizing unity, authority, and readiness for peace).
- **Key Dates & Mintages:**
  - 1916-D: **264,000** (Undisputed key of the series; struck at Denver).
  - 1921: 1,230,000 (Low mintage recession year).
  - 1921-D: 1,080,000 (Low mintage recession year).
- **Major Documented Varieties (Type/Variety Rows):**
  - 1942/1 Philadelphia Overdate (struck from doubled hub with 1941 and 1942 impressions).
  - 1942/1-D Denver Overdate.
  - 1945-S Micro S (repunched small mint mark punch from Philippine coinage stock).
- **Catalog Note:** Full Split Bands (FSB / FB) is a strike/grade designation, not a distinct type; catalog maintains standard type and date/mint rows.

### A2: Walking Liberty Half Dollars (1916–1947)
- **Designer:** Adolph A. Weinman.
- **Obverse:** Full-length figure of Liberty striding toward sunrise, enveloped in folds of the Stars and Stripes, holding laurel and oak branches.
- **Reverse:** American bald eagle perched on a mountain pine cliff with wings outstretched.
- **Mint Mark Placements:**
  - 1916–1917: Mint mark (D or S) on **Obverse** below motto IN GOD WE TRUST.
  - 1917–1947: Mint mark moved to **Reverse** at lower left near rim below pine branch (Act of Feb 14, 1917 design adjustment to avoid obverse die wear).
- **Key Dates & Scarcity Rows:**
  - 1921: 246,000 (Philadelphia key).
  - 1921-D: 208,000 (Lowest regular mintage of series).
  - 1921-S: 542,000 (Extremely elusive in higher grades).
  - 1938-D: 491,600 (Key modern date; low depression mintage).

### A3: Franklin Half Dollars (1948–1963)
- **Designer:** John R. Sinnock.
- **Obverse:** Bust of Benjamin Franklin facing right; first regular-issue non-presidential American citizen depicted on circulating US coinage.
- **Reverse:** Liberty Bell with small heraldic eagle at right (to comply with statutory requirement under Act of 1873 requiring eagle on coins above 25¢).
- **Key Dates:**
  - 1949-S: 3,744,000.
  - 1953-S: 4,148,000.
  - 1955: 2,496,000 ("Bugs Bunny" die clash variety — obverse die clashed with eagle's wings on reverse, creating appearance of buck teeth on Franklin's upper lip).
- **Catalog Rule Compliance:** Full Bell Lines (FBL) treated as condition attribute, not separate catalog type row.

### A4: Eisenhower Dollars (1971–1978)
- **Designer:** Frank Gasparro.
- **Compositional Split:**
  - Cupro-Nickel Clad (Circulation & Uncirculated sets: 75% Cu, 25% Ni on 100% Cu core).
  - 40% Silver Clad (Collector Proof/Uncirculated San Francisco issues: 80% Ag outer layer on 20.9% Ag inner core, net 0.3161 troy oz silver).
- **Reverse Types & Varieties:**
  - 1972 Type 1: Low relief, flat Earth with 3 islands off Florida.
  - 1972 Type 2: High relief reverse hub (modified from 1971 proof dies; single incuse crater line, Florida as triangular smudge, no islands). Key variety.
  - 1972 Type 3: Normal high relief reverse with 3 distinct islands.
  - 1976 Bicentennial: Dual-dated 1776-1976.
    - *Type 1 (1975 strikes):* Broad, bold sans-serif lettering on reverse (STATES OF AMERICA).
    - *Type 2 (1976 strikes):* Delicate, thin serif lettering on reverse with improved design definition.

### A5: Susan B. Anthony Dollars (1979–1981, 1999)
- **Designer:** Frank Gasparro.
- **Significance:** First circulating US coin honoring an actual real American woman. Hendecagonal (11-sided) inner border to differentiate from quarter.
- **Key Varieties & Dates:**
  - 1979-P Wide Rim / Near Date: Rim is substantially wider, placing the date numerals 1979 extremely close to the inner border (subsequent 1980 modification).
  - 1979-S Type 1 vs. Type 2 Proof: Type 1 Filled S mint mark; Type 2 Clear/Bulbous S mint mark.
  - 1981-S Type 1 vs. Type 2 Proof: Type 1 Flat S; Type 2 Clear, round serifs on S.
  - 1999-P / 1999-D: Re-authorized for vending and mass transit supply prior to the introduction of the Sacagawea Golden Dollar.

### A7: Barber Quarters (Liberty Head, 1892–1916)
- **Designer:** Charles E. Barber.
- **Obverse:** Classical Liberty head facing right wearing Phrygian cap and laurel wreath, ribbon inscribed LIBERTY.
- **Reverse:** Heraldic eagle clutching arrows and olive branch, 13 stars above eagle head, cloud canopy.
- **Key Dates & Mintages:**
  - 1896-S: **188,039** (Key).
  - 1901-S: **72,664** (Premier key of series, exceptional rarity in all grades).
  - 1913-S: **40,000** (Lowest regular business strike mintage in 20th-century US silver coinage).
