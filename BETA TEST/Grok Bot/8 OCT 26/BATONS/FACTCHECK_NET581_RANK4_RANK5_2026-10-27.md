## 1. Executive verdict

### **PASS WITH CORRECTIONS**

Rank 4 type frames for Half Cents, Large Cents (incl. **15-link Chain Cent**), Two-Cent (first IGWT + 1864 Small/Large Motto), Trimes Types 1–3, Three-Cent Nickel, Twenty-Cent, Half Dimes, early dollars / Gobrecht / Seated subtypes / Trade Dollars, and pre-1933 gold types are **largely Supported** at type-set level. The only explicit mintage integer (**1794 dollar 1,758**) is a **Tier A Mint delivery figure** — not invented. No freestyle invented mintages found.

Rank 5 US-PH M8a–c (Act of Mar 2, 1903; P/S/Manila; Figueroa/Barber; denom list; Commonwealth 1935–1945; Victory notes; post-1946 OUT) is **largely Supported** with several corrections before any later merge.

**Blocking / high-priority corrections before any later merge:**

1. **Gold denomination blanks / typos** — Draft headers show **“Quarter Eagles (.50…)”**, **“Half Eagles (.00…)”**, **“Eagles (.00…)”**, **“Double Eagles (.00…)”**. These are corrupted denomination fields. Fix to **$2.50 / $5 / $10 / $20**.
2. **PH 20¢ / 50¢ fineness incomplete** — Draft states both as “Silver (0.750 fine)” only. **1903–1906 were .900 fine** (larger/heavier); **.750 reduced size/weight begins 1907** (same Act/weight reform that cut the peso). Mirror the Ten Centavos / One Peso reduction note.
3. **Manila “M” mintmark** — Manila Mint **opened 1920**, but **1920–1922 Manila issues carry no mintmark**; **M appears from 1925**. Do not equate “opened 1920” with “M from 1920.”
4. **Designer spelling** — **Melecio** Figueroa (not “Melicio”).
5. **Half Cent Liberty Cap engraver** — Soften/replace “Joseph Wright and Robert Scot.” **1793 left-facing** is more credibly **Henry Voigt** (Wright attribution weakly supported / disputed); **1794+ right-facing** = **Robert Scot** (Gardner assists later small head).

**Also fix / soften:** 1804 Class wording (“fantasies”); Seated 1873–74 “Trade-weight” label; Trade Dollar 1884–85 clandestine proofs; Braided Hair **1852 proof-only** exception; 1936 commemorative three-coin set wording; guerilla “authorized by MacArthur” overclaim; One Centavo 1903–1936 territorial date straddle.

**Thin packs — do NOT force Supported:** Full die-variety depth (Sheldon/Newcomb/Breen); Classic Head cent “softer copper” as composition claim; Gobrecht die-alignment census; full Seated subtype matrix per denomination; pre-1933 gold die varieties; PH guerilla island-by-island census; any invented PH mintages (none present — keep that way).
