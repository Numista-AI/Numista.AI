# Numista.Ai: Investor Deck Market & Competitor Data
_Compiled November 5, 2026 (ET) from public web pages, App Store and Google Play listings, press releases, SEC filings and news coverage. Nobody was contacted and no accounts were created._

**Rules used in this report**
- Every figure has a source link next to it.
- **UNKNOWN** means no public figure was found. Nothing was filled in by guessing.
- **ESTIMATE** means I calculated the number myself. The method is written next to it.
- ⚠️ **WEAK** marks a number that is old, comes from a secondary source, or is a self-reported or vaguely defined claim.
- App Store rating counts come from Apple's public iTunes lookup API, pulled 2026-11-05. Google Play install bands and review counts come from the public Play listing pages, pulled the same day. Both are snapshots and will drift.

**ESTIMATE: serviceable US market for a paid coin-collection app**
- **Method:** 250K–500K active US collectors (ANA blog range) × an assumed **$40–$60/yr** price (in line with CoinSnap's $39.99/yr and Collectr's $59.99/yr) = **about $10M–$30M a year if every active collector paid**.
- This is a ceiling for the core segment only. It leaves out the much larger casual and inheritor segment (38% "ever collected").

**ESTIMATE: reachable casual segment (do not use as a headline)**
- **Method:** about 262M US adults (rounded; Census 2024 ≈ 262M, not opened this run) × 38% "ever collected" ≈ **100M people**.
- Treat this as a TAM-style upper bound only. The underlying survey is weak.

## 5. Flags for the deck
1. Use the **US Mint FY2025 numbers ($490.1M, 2.9M units)** as the anchor market stat. They are audited and recent.
2. Use the **250K–500K active collectors** figure only as an expert-estimate range, and cite the ANA blog.
3. Avoid presenting **"$6B US rare coin market"** as fact. Cite it as a dealer estimate, if you use it at all.
4. CoinSnap's 10M+ Android installs and 310K iOS ratings show that consumer demand for coin ID exists, without any VC.
5. **Collectr** (8-figure ARR, $500K raised, $7.99/mo) is the best evidence that a lean, subscription-based collection tracker can scale.
6. The NGC app's ratings (2.1★ on iOS, 2.3★ on Android) show that the grading incumbents' consumer UX is a weak spot.
